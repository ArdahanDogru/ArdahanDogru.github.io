Privacy policy: Hawk uploader

Last updated 28 October 2026.

What it can access

Only the Google Drive files and folders it creates itself (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see, change or delete any other file in the account.

What it stores

Inspection images and backups of inspection results, uploaded to the owner's own Google Drive. It reads no other data from the Google account: no email, contacts, calendar or profile information.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. The data stays in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's server.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/ArdahanDogru.
